Mohammed Siraj

is a talented fast bowler of India. He was born on 13 March 1994 in Hyderabad, Telangana. He is a right-arm fast bowler known for his speed and aggression. Siraj worked very hard from a young age to achieve success in cricket. He first impressed everyone in domestic cricket and later in the Indian Premier League. His strong performances helped him earn a place in the Indian team. Siraj is known for taking important wickets, especially in Test matches. He bowls with great energy and confidence. Despite facing many challenges in life, he never gave up. His dedication and fighting spirit make him an important player for Indian cricket.
