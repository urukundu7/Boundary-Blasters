Varun Chakaravarthy

varun chakaravarthy is a talented spin bowler of India. He was born on 29 August 1991 in Tamil Nadu. He is known as a mystery spinner because his bowling variations confuse batsmen. Varun bowls leg-spin and other different types of deliveries. He first became popular in domestic cricket and later in the Indian Premier League. His impressive performances in the IPL helped him get selected for the Indian team. He is known for controlling runs and taking important wickets. Varun Chakravarthy’s hard work and unique bowling style make him a valuable player for Indian cricket.
